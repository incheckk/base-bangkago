How to confirm this downpayment
  1. Open the passenger's receipt image below and read the GCash Ref No.
  2. In your GCash app open Transactions and search that Ref No. (or match the amount + date).
  3. Confirm the money landed in your GCash number (Admin → GCash QR) and the amount matches the row.
  4. Money found → Approve. Not found / wrong amount → Refund.
