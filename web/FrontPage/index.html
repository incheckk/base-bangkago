Passenger Manual
Ride, Send & Explore with BangkaGo

New to BangkaGo? Follow these six steps and you'll be on the water in no time.

  1. 1
    Create your account

    Open the BangkaGo app and sign up with your Philippine mobile number (09XX). No email address needed.

  2. 2
    Choose your trip

    Book a boat ride (solo or group), send a parcel with Padala, reserve an Island Hopping package, or rent a boat. Pick your departure port, destination, and sail date — today or up to 14 days ahead — then add your travel companions.

  3. 3
    Pay securely

    Same-day trips are paid on board. For advance bookings, send a GCash downpayment to the official BangkaGo QR, upload your receipt in the app, and our admin confirms it (held in escrow) to lock in your seat.

  4. 4
    Get matched in real time

    Your request is sent instantly to every available bangkero. You'll get a notification the moment one accepts your booking.

  5. 5
    Board with your QR

    Open My Bookings and show your boarding-pass QR at the pier; the bangkero scans it to tick you aboard. While sailing, follow your boat live on the map with status and ETA updates.

  6. 6
    Arrive, settle, and rate

    Pay any remaining balance on board when you arrive, then rate your bangkero after the trip to help keep the fleet safe and trusted.

One active booking per day per passenger — plan ahead for the whole family by adding companions to a single booking.

Bangkero Manual
Accept Trips & Earn with BangkaGo

Ready to bring passengers and parcels across the water? Here's everything you need to start earning as a certified bangkero.

  1. 1
    Register as a bangkero

    Sign up in the app with your mobile number and upload your government ID, boat registration, and required permits from your profile.

  2. 2
    Get verified

    The BangkaGo admin reviews your documents. Once approved, you're notified right away and your account goes online.

  3. 3
    Set up your boat

    Register your bangka (name, capacity, and permits), keep your availability toggle on while you want trips, and optionally list your boat for rentals.

  4. 4
    Accept requests live

    Booking requests appear on your home screen in real time with the route, passenger count, and fare. Accept or pass — the first bangkero to accept gets the booking.

  5. 5
    Run the trip

    Ready to Depart → Depart → Sail. Scan each passenger's boarding QR and review the manifest before leaving the port; passengers watch your live position on the way. Tap I have arrived, then Complete Trip at the destination.

  6. 6
    Earn and build your rating

    Collect the balance on board, track your completed trips and earnings in the app, and grow your bangkero rating with every passenger review.

Tip: complete your documents and keep your availability on to receive more requests — passengers book up to 14 days ahead.
