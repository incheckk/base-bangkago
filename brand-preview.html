Two directions · same boat

Same boat. Two frames.

V1 is your PNG traced 1:1 — corners rounded, grey bars gone, the sail on the app's teal. V2 leaves that boat exactly as it is and changes the frame instead: a cream seal with a thin teal ring, the wordmark stacked underneath, and a navy icon tile. Same hull, same sail, same outrigger, same three colours. Shipped as three assets — the bare mark, the cream tile and the seal — each used where it reads.

4 · What actually changes

V1 · the mark

  • No frame. The boat is the logo; a cream squircle appears only where the ground is dark.
  • Horizontal lockup. Badge on the left, wordmark on the right.
  • Light icon. Cream tile — bright on the home screen.
  • Feels like: a sticker. Friendly, app-native, the thing you already approved.

V2 · the seal

  • A ring, not a tile. Cream disc with a thin teal ring; the boat sits inside it untouched.
  • Stacked lockup. Wordmark centred underneath — a crest, not a banner.
  • Dark icon. Navy tile with the cream seal — calm next to other apps.
  • Feels like: an emblem. Sober, official, the thing a coast guard would stamp.
Identical in both: the hull, the sail, the outrigger, the float and their coordinates; the three colours #0D3346 / #34D6B0 / #FFC24D; the rounding (hull 26, sail peak 22); and no grey bars. V2 moves nothing — it only puts a frame around it.

5 · Redraws I tried, and why V2 is a frame instead

Wind-filled lateenAt 48px the billow closes up and the sail reads as a fin.

Curved keelYour trapezoid is what makes it a hull; curved, it turns into a leaf and the stern disappears.

Centred triangle sailSymmetric, it reads as a mountain on a boat — the flat top of your sail is what says "sail".

7 · Animation — preview only

A · Moored
recommended

The boat rests. The float drifts ±6 and tilts 1.2°; the hull follows 0.35 s behind at ±3.5. A 4.2 s loop. Nothing spins, nothing bounces — it reads as breathing.

B · Under way
loader only

Same motion, plus water. Two thin teal waves slide under the float. Busier — it belongs in the web loader, which already draws waves, not on the app's welcome screen.

Both stop dead under prefers-reduced-motion. This is a preview — the app and the web are not animated yet. If you want it, the app animates the same two groups with Reanimated (already installed, no new dependency) and the web loader takes concept B.
